# ADR 002: One poller fetches every feed on a fixed schedule

> Superseded on 2026-10-19 by [ADR 004](004.md): each host now has its own fetch queue. This record is kept as written, to show what was decided in March.

## Context and Problem Statement

Feeds change at any time and most give no notice. Inside one process (ADR 001), what fetches them, and when?

## Decision Drivers

- A new article shows up within 15 minutes.
- Fetching must fit in the one process.

## Considered Options

1. One poller thread that walks every feed in turn, every 15 minutes
2. A timer per feed

## Decision Outcome

Chosen: option 1, because one loop is the least code that meets the 15-minute driver, and a walk of the maintainer's 2,400 feeds took 6 minutes. Option 2 lost because 2,400 timers gave no way to bound how many fetches ran at once.

Confidence: high that it works at 2,400 feeds. Unknown beyond that.

### Consequences

- Good, because there is one place to look when fetching stops.
- Bad, because one slow host delays every feed after it in the walk.
- Bad, because feeds on the same host are fetched back to back, in a burst.

### Confirmation

`tests/test_poller.py` fails if a walk of 100 local feeds starts a second thread.
